# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation harassment-free regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable technical feedback, and
protect private information. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment,
personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, or
other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer. Maintainers may
remove content, reject contributions, or restrict participation.
